## 1. Toolchain rebuilt and validated — and the recipe in the register is wrong

fpocket 4.2.3 rebuilt from source. **The recorded recipe does not build as
written.** Two corrections:

- `-Isrc/qhull/src` belongs in **`QCFLAGS`**, not `CFLAGS`. The qhull objects
  compile under a separate rule (`$(CCQHULL) $(QCFLAGS)`), so putting it in
  `CFLAGS` never reaches them.
- Overriding `CFLAGS` wholesale **clobbers the makefile's own**
  `-Iplugins/include -Iplugins/LINUXAMD64/molfile`, and the build then dies on
  `libmolfile_plugin.h`. Those must be re-added.

Working invocation:

```
make CFLAGS="-O2 -std=gnu99 -I$S/src/qhull/src -I$S/src/headers -I$S/headers \
             -I$S/plugins/include -I$S/plugins/LINUXAMD64/molfile" \
     QCFLAGS="-O2 -I$S/src/qhull/src"
```

**Validation, both stages exact:**

- regenerated apo inputs match `expA_fpocket.json` residue **and** atom counts,
  **20/20**
- fpocket reproduces `hyd_cache.json` candidate counts, **40/40** across both
  candidate settings

`fp_full.json` now holds all 40 runs *with alpha-sphere coordinates*, which
`hyd_cache.json` lacked and which every ENM experiment needs.

## 2. Experiment B arms 2/3 — conservation. Negative, and informative.

Run exactly as pre-registered: UniProt → InterPro Pfam → seed alignment + family
HMM → hmmalign seed to its own HMM (match states off the RF line) →
Jensen-Shannon (Capra & Singh) with Henikoff weights → mapped to auth residue
numbers. 20 chains, Pfam coverage 0.26–0.96.

- **Arm 2:** conservation never ranks the true pocket #1 — 0/6 open, 0/5 cryptic,
  at either setting. Cluster-collapsed Fisher p 0.45–0.98. In the cryptic
  stratum the true pocket is *more* conserved than two-thirds of competitors,
  the opposite of the pre-registered direction.
- **Arm 3:** as a negative filter on hydrophobic density, parameter-free Borda,
  rank-1 goes **7/11 → 0/11**. Not dilution — destruction.

**The instrument works** (this is why the null counts): every textbook catalytic
residue lands at percentile 0.83–0.99 — HCV NS5B GDD motif 0.99, TrpA Glu49
0.96, KSHV Ser23 nucleophile 0.86. Residue-level AUC reproduces the register's
existing negative at 0.46/0.52.

**The test had power**: at K=4–13 it would have detected a filter placing the
true pocket in the top 14–22% of candidates. Observed medians 0.33–0.69.

**Record this as a measured negative.** The memo that the residue-level Shapley
result "should not be treated as a settled negative" was correct as an
objection — the pocket-level framing has now been run and it fails on its own
terms.

**Free confirmation of arm 1:** re-derived under max-Jaccard truth (the
definition that collapsed volume and SASA to chance), hydrophobic density holds
at **6/6 open, 2/6 druggability** — identical to the old table, different code
path, size-neutral truth. Arm 1 survives the correction.

## 3. Experiment D — ENM mode shift. Real, but not where it was wanted.

Open stratum, cluster-collapsed, **survives fixed-node-budget AND
volume-residualisation AND both candidate settings**: p = 0.005–0.013. Not a
size artifact.

Cryptic stratum: not significant. p = 0.15–0.65.

**The review's premise — that mode shift is the component that works where
hydrophobicity fails — is not supported.** It works in the same stratum
hydrophobicity already solved.

Three things worth carrying forward:

1. **Mode shift is more robust than hydrophobic density to candidate-set
   expansion.** Going default → `-m 2.8`, hyd's open rank-1 falls 6/6 → 3/6
   while mode shift's p is unmoved (0.0052 → 0.0060). Since §6 recommends
   adopting `-m 2.8`, this affects which feature to build on.
2. **The APOP combination does not reproduce.** The two are largely independent
   (ρ = 0.07 / 0.48) but Borda never beats the better component (7/11 → 4/11).
   A weighted combination might, but weighting is fitting, and K_eff = 4–6
   cannot support a believable weight. The 92/104 claim is neither confirmed nor
   refuted — only its parameter-free form is refuted.
3. **The cryptic result is suggestive, not null.** Median percentile 0.24–0.28
   against a K=6 detection boundary of 0.173 — right side of chance, cannot
   clear the bar. Contrast conservation at 0.66–0.69 on the wrong side. This is
   **the first candidate cryptic signal in the register**, and the cohort cannot
   resolve it.

Caveat: uniform GNM 7.3 Å was used. The repo's per-target `enm_cutoff` from
`build_H_new` was not available in this container and should be tried before the
cryptic conclusion is trusted.

## 4. Sibling conformers (§6 item 6) — feasible, with one trap

Scanned `search.rcsb.org` by sequence identity. **Every target has siblings:
median 53 entries at ≥95% identity, minimum 10, none below 5.** The experiment
is fully runnable with no MD and no Docker.

**The trap:** most siblings are holo structures, and some will contain the drug
ligand itself. Scoring "persistence of a pocket across conformers" over a set
that includes structures solved *with the allosteric drug bound* is the same
self-fulfilling error §5 flags for the matched holo control — the cavity was
imprinted by the very ligand being predicted. **Filter siblings by bound ligand
before scoring**, at minimum excluding the target's own `drug_ligand`, and
report results both with and without apo-only restriction.
