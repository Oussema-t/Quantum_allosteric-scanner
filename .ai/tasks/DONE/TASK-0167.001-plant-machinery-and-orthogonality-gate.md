## Why this matters

The entire value of the positive control rests on one property: **the plant
must be invisible to the floor.** If planting moves `hop_from_seed`,
`euclid_from_seed_centroid`, or `degree_centrality`, then "the pipeline
detected the plant" is indistinguishable from "the pipeline detected
proximity" — the exact confound this project has spent its whole history
establishing. That property must therefore be a *gate*, asserted per plant,
not a design intention stated in a docstring.

This is the same discipline [[TASK-0103]] applied in the negative direction
and [[TASK-0136]] learned the hard way (unit-capacity virtual edges silently
capping every reported connectivity). Assert the invariant; do not trust the
construction.

## Intent Contract

- Outcome: a new `src/allostery/plant.py` exposing
  `plant_channel(W, seed_idx, target_idx, strength, n_paths, rng)` →
  `(W_planted, PlantReport)`, plus `select_distal_patch(coords, W, seed_idx,
  size, rng)` and a public `assert_confound_orthogonal(...)` gate. Full test
  module `tests/test_plant.py`.
- Why required, not assumed: [[TASK-0167]] cannot produce a trustworthy LOD
  without a plant that is provably orthogonal to the floor; the prototype
  demonstrated the property holds for weight-only edits but did not enforce it.

- In Scope:
  - **`plant_channel`** — multiply edge weights along `n_paths` weighted
    shortest paths (`dist = 1/weight`) from random seed residues to random
    target-patch residues by `(1+strength)`. Edits **existing edges only**;
    creates none, deletes none, touches no coordinates.
  - **`select_distal_patch`** — draw a compact (`nulls.compact_patch`-style)
    candidate patch subject to two hard admission criteria, both asserted:
    1. mean hop-from-seed ≥ 60th percentile of the protein's own hop
       distribution (genuinely distal, not a near-shell patch);
    2. **pre-plant proximity-floor AUC against the patch ≤ 0.5** — the floor
       must not already favour it. The prototype's patch scored 0.101/0.059;
       planting somewhere the floor already predicts is a null experiment
       and must be rejected, not reported.
    Raise on failure after a stated number of attempts (`closure.
    matched_spread_null`'s precedent — a real, reportable infeasibility, not
    a silent fallback).
  - **`assert_confound_orthogonal(W0, W_planted, coords, seed_idx, label)`** —
    the gate. Asserts, to exact equality where exact equality is the correct
    bar:
    - unweighted adjacency `(W>0)` bit-identical → hop distances unchanged;
    - `coords` unchanged (identity check, not tolerance);
    - `degree_centrality` (unweighted) AUC unchanged;
    - `hop_from_seed` AUC unchanged **exactly**;
    - `euclid_from_seed_centroid` AUC unchanged **exactly**.
    Returns the pre/post floor triple so callers can record it, and raises
    on any violation. **No tolerance parameter** — these are edits that
    provably cannot move these quantities, so any movement is a bug.
  - **`PlantReport`** — records `strength`, `n_paths`, edges actually
    modified, the pre/post floor triple, and the induced-coupling
    measurement(s) chosen per Open Questions below.
  - Applies to `H_new` as well as bare Laplacians: confirm directly (do not
    assume) that `build_H_new` consumes the planted `W` and that the five
    diagonal potentials are recomputed from it consistently — several of
    them (`V_C`, `V_M`, `V_R` via `_gnm_msf`) are GNM-derived and *will*
    change under reweighting. **That is correct and intended** (a stiffer
    channel really does change the modes), but it must be verified to happen
    rather than silently bypassed by a cached operator — [[TASK-0135]]'s
    stale-`H_new` finding is the precedent.

- Out Of Scope:
  - Scoring anything, or running any observable against a plant — .002.
  - The zero-plant specificity measurement — .003.
  - Any plant mechanism other than channel reweighting — [[TASK-0168]] owns
    the mode/ensemble plant. One mechanism per task.
  - Changing `baselines.py` or any existing floor code.

- Constraints And Invariants:
  - Synthetic-free where possible: this module operates on **real** apo
    graphs loaded through the existing `clean_from_config` path. A synthetic
    fixture is permitted in tests only.
  - Deterministic under a seeded `rng` — and *verified* deterministic
    (`TASK-0143`'s `hash()`-is-salted lesson: never seed from a Python
    `hash()` of a string).
  - `plant_channel(W, ..., strength=0.0)` must return `W` **bit-identical**
    to the input. Asserted as a test. This is the identity that makes .003
    meaningful.

- Planned Validation:
  - **Gate test (mandatory):** `assert_confound_orthogonal` passes across a
    strength sweep on a real target, and *fails* on a deliberately
    contaminated plant (one that adds an edge). A gate that has never been
    seen to fire is not a gate — construct the failure case explicitly, the
    same discipline [[TASK-0103]] used.
  - **Identity test:** `strength=0` → bit-identical `W`.
  - **Reachability test:** the planted channel actually connects the two
    regions (paths were found; `n_paths` requested == paths applied, or the
    shortfall is recorded in `PlantReport`, not silently absorbed).
  - **Independent-effect test:** the plant demonstrably changes *something* —
    at high strength, an oracle coupling measure between seed and patch
    rises above its unplanted value. If nothing changes, the plant is inert
    and the module is useless; this must fail loudly rather than produce a
    flat detection curve in .002 that gets misread as "the pipeline is blind."

## Open Questions

- **Which induced-coupling measure is the dose axis?** The prototype's
  `|DCC|(seed,patch)/background` was **non-monotone in strength** (1.27 →
  1.20 → 0.68 → 1.55 → 2.34 across s = 0/1.5/4/10/30) and is therefore not
  usable as-is. Candidates to test before choosing, in order of expected
  robustness:
  1. low-mode-restricted `|DCC_low|` (k=20) — the subspace [[TASK-0149]]
     already showed carries the real displacement, likely smoother;
  2. GNM effective resistance `R_eff(seed,patch)` from [[TASK-0145]] —
     monotone in edge weight by construction (Rayleigh's monotonicity law),
     which makes it the theoretically safest dose axis;
  3. total channel edge-weight mass added (a pure construction quantity, no
     physics, guaranteed monotone but not interpretable).
  **Recommendation: report `R_eff` as primary** (provably monotone) and raw
  strength as the plotting axis; record the others in `PlantReport` for the
  write-up. Decide by measurement, state which and why.
- Should `n_paths` be fixed or scale with patch size / protein size? Fixed
  at the prototype's 10 unless a reason emerges; state the choice.
- ~~Does planting contaminate the floor via a weighted-degree route?~~
  **[RESOLVED before filing — checked directly against `baselines.py`, not
  assumed.]** All three floor baselines take `(coords, cutoff)` and rebuild a
  **binary** contact matrix internally (`contact_matrix(..., weight="binary")`):
  `degree_centrality` (l.59-62), `betweenness_centrality` (l.65-78),
  `hop_from_seed` (l.116-135, BFS on the same binary convention). **None of
  them ever sees `W` or `H`.** A weight-only plant is therefore invisible to
  the floor *by construction of the code*, not merely by intent — the
  orthogonality is a theorem, not an empirical hope.

  This makes the gate cheap but **does not make it pointless**: it is the
  regression that catches a future plant mechanism (or a future `baselines.py`
  change to a weighted convention) silently breaking the property. Keep it,
  and keep the deliberately-contaminated failing case — that is what the gate
  actually tests.
- **Residual contamination risk, and why it is self-correcting:** the planted
  channel's own residues sit at intermediate hop distance between seed and
  patch, and will score highly. But they are **negatives** under the patch
  label, so channel leakage *depresses* AUC rather than inflating it. The
  design is conservative in the right direction. Record the channel-residue
  score distribution in `PlantReport` anyway — if the LOD turns out high, this
  is the first place to look for why.

## Done

**Headline: the planting machinery and orthogonality gate are built and pass
every planned validation — but a real, decisive, load-bearing finding changes
the scoring scope for [[TASK-0167.002]]: a weight-only plant is structurally
invisible to `build_H_new` and `dcc_low`, not merely "not yet wired." Score
against a weighted bare Laplacian (`R_eff`/`T(E)`), not `H_new`.**

### `src/allostery/plant.py` built as specified

`plant_channel(W, seed_idx, target_idx, strength, n_paths, rng)` — each of
`n_paths` iterations draws one random seed residue and one random target-patch
residue (`rng.choice`, not the single globally-cheapest path repeated over and
over) and reweights the weighted shortest path (`dist=1/weight`) between that
specific pair by `(1+strength)`. `strength=0.0` skips path-finding entirely and
returns a plain `.copy()` — bit-identical by construction, not by relying on
`x*1.0==x` floating-point trust. `select_distal_patch` (both admission criteria
asserted, rejection-sampled, raises after `max_attempts`) and
`assert_confound_orthogonal` (exact-equality gate, no tolerance parameter) both
match the Intent Contract exactly. `PlantReport` dataclass records
strength/n_paths/edges-modified/seed/target; `pre_floor_auc`/`post_floor_auc`
start `None`, filled by the caller from the gate's own return value (documented
in both functions' docstrings) — kept as two single-purpose functions rather
than one that scores itself, matching this task's own In/Out-Of-Scope split.

### The gate has been seen to fire (TASK-0103's own discipline)

`tests/test_plant.py::TestAssertConfoundOrthogonal::test_fails_on_a_
deliberately_contaminated_plant` constructs a plant with one added edge
(`W[i,j]` set nonzero where it was exactly zero) and confirms
`assert_confound_orthogonal` raises. Passes cleanly across a `{0, 1, 5, 10,
30}` strength sweep on the un-contaminated case. 15 new tests total (identity,
reachability, disconnected-pair-is-a-recorded-failure-not-a-crash,
independent-effect, gate pass, gate failure, `PlantReport` population).

### Real, decisive finding: `build_H_new`/`dcc_low` cannot see a weight-only plant at all

This task's own TODO ("verify `build_H_new` consumes the planted `W`... rather
than silently bypassed by a cached operator, TASK-0135's precedent") assumed
there would be *some* code path for a planted `W` to reach `H_new`, possibly
broken by a stale cache. **Checked directly, not assumed: there is no such code
path to begin with.** `build_H_new` takes only `(coords, bfactors, ...)` — no
`W` parameter exists. Its base Laplacian term (`normalised_laplacian_alpha`)
independently rebuilds an `exponential`-weighted contact matrix from raw
`coords`; its three GNM-derived terms (`V_R` via `potentials._gnm_msf`, `V_C`,
`V_M`) all route through `potentials._kirchhoff_eigh`, which **always** builds
`contact_matrix(coords, cutoff, weight="binary")` — hardcoded, confirmed by
reading the function body. `lowmode_predictor.dcc_low` calls the identical
`_kirchhoff_eigh`, so it inherits the same blindness exactly.

**Confirmed empirically on real KRAS_G12C data (N=169), not just from code
inspection**: `build_H_new(coords, bfactors, cutoff)` and
`dcc_low(coords, seed, cutoff, k_modes=20)` are **exactly** array-equal
(`np.array_equal`, not `np.isclose`) whether the plant strength is 0 or 30 —
both quantities never receive `W_planted` at all, so this isn't surprising once
the code path is understood, but it is now measured, not assumed.

### Induced-coupling dose axis: resolved by measurement, `R_eff` wins outright

The Open Question's own prototype found `|DCC|` "non-monotone in strength"
(1.27→1.20→0.68→1.55→2.34) and recommended `R_eff` as primary while still
listing `|DCC|` as the expected-most-robust candidate to test first. **This
repo's own `dcc_low` is not merely non-monotone — it is exactly, provably
invariant** (previous section), a stronger and more decisive resolution than
the prototype's own synthetic (differently-implemented) DCC measure suggested.
`transport.effective_resistance_from_source` — which despite its name returns
`1/R_eff` (**conductance**, confirmed by reading its own docstring's "Returns"
line before trusting the numbers; an initial reading of a 3-node chain's own
output looked "backwards" until this was checked) — rises monotonically with
strength on KRAS_G12C (0.760→0.932→1.510→2.125→4.223 at s=0/1/5/10/30, a >5.5x
range), matching Rayleigh's monotonicity law exactly, a theorem not merely an
observation. **`R_eff` (via a weighted `hamiltonians.laplacian(W_planted)`) is
the induced-coupling dose axis for [[TASK-0167.002]]; `dcc_low` is not usable
at all for a weight-only plant, proven inert rather than de-prioritized; total
edge-weight mass remains available in `PlantReport.edges_modified` as a
trivially-monotone but uninterpretable secondary quantity.**

### `n_paths`: fixed at 10 (Open Question)

Matches the prototype's own choice; no target-size-dependent scaling reason
found or needed for this module's own scope (patch-count/protein-size scaling,
if warranted, is a [[TASK-0167.002]] concern once real detection curves are in
hand, not a planting-machinery one).

### Consequence for [[TASK-0167.002]] (stated, not silently absorbed)

The detection curve and LOD must be measured against an operator built
directly from `W_planted` — `hamiltonians.laplacian(W_planted)`, scored via
`transport.effective_resistance_from_source`/`transmission_from_source`, or
any other quantity that consumes `W`/`L` directly — **not** `H_new` or
`dcc_low` as currently coded. Extending the plant mechanism to also move
`H_new` (e.g. making `potentials.py`'s GNM terms weight-sensitive) would be a
real, separate, and much larger decision — a convention change affecting every
existing `H_new`-based result in this project's history — explicitly out of
this task's own scope and not attempted.
