**2026-08-22/23, Implementer D.**

**Citation verified directly, before implementing against it**: Zheng
(2023), *J. Chem. Phys.* 158:124127, DOI 10.1063/5.0141630, "Predicting
allosteric sites using fast conformational sampling as guided by
coarse-grained normal modes" — confirmed exactly (title/journal/volume/
issue/page/DOI). Method confirmed from the abstract/summary: for each of
the lowest ~30 ANM modes, sample along *that single mode* and check
whether pocket-finding software detects a cryptic site in the deformed
structure — a per-mode deterministic scan, **not** a joint Boltzmann-
ensemble draw over all modes at once (which is what this project's
existing `allostery.shortcuts.equipartition_ensemble`/[[TASK-0185]] does
— a different, already-answered question, not this method).

**Implementation, reusing extensive existing infrastructure, not
re-deriving it**: new `scripts/task0229_004_zheng_nma_baseline.py`.
- `allostery.superpose.anm_modes` for the ANM eigendecomposition
  (unmodified, shared with the rest of the project).
- `conformational_search_measurement`'s (TASK-0185) own
  `_apply_ca_displacement`/`_load_full_atom_template`/
  `_pockets_overlap_frac`, imported not copied.
- `task0163_external_baseline_scoring`'s (TASK-0163) already-vendored,
  already-tested `_run_fpocket`/`_write_full_atom_apo_pdb`/`FPOCKET_BIN`
  — confirmed working directly (`tools/fpocket/bin/fpocket`) before
  relying on it.
- `allostery.plant.select_distal_patch` (TASK-0167.001) for the
  negative-control decoy.
- `allostery.nulls.graph_walk_patch_matched` (TASK-0201's corrected
  compact-patch null, this task's own Constraint's named convention) for
  the significance test.
- The one genuinely new piece: `per_mode_displacements` — for each of
  `N_MODES=20` modes, 6 amplitudes (±1, ±2, ±3 multiples of that mode's
  own thermal scale `sqrt(kT/eigval)`), a deterministic grid stated as an
  **Implementer's-call choice**, not asserted as literally matching
  Zheng's own protocol (the paper's own exact amplitude-scan recipe was
  not available beyond its abstract/summary) — `N_MODES=20` similarly
  stated as a compute-budget compromise against the paper's own "lowest
  30," not a literal match.
- Residue-level score = fraction of (mode, amplitude) samples in which
  that residue falls inside *any* fpocket-detected cavity — a natural
  per-residue generalization of Zheng's own pocket-level hit-rate
  statistic, computed in the same fpocket pass as that statistic (no
  duplicate fpocket calls).

**Planned Validation (this task's own Constraint) — passed.** BCR_ABL1
(this register's own established ENM-mode-driven positive control,
[[TASK-0185]]) used as the practical stand-in for reproducing a
qualitative claim from the paper (an explicit judgment call, stated as
such — Zheng's own 4 worked systems were not available to reproduce
directly here): real-pocket hit rate 92.5% vs. decoy hit rate 36.7%
across 120 samples, 0 fpocket errors — the per-mode sampler shows real,
pocket-specific opening, not generic conformational noise, before being
trusted on the scored VALID targets. Confirmed via `main()`'s own
explicit gate (scoring the VALID targets is skipped if this check fails
— not just eyeballed after the fact).

**Scored results, [[TASK-0209]]'s VALID targets only**:

- **KRAS_G12C**: whole-graph AUC **0.728** vs. proximity floor **0.530**
  (`hop_from_seed`) — a wide point-estimate margin, and **substantially
  above this register's own quantum-observable AUC on the same target**
  (0.557-0.590 depending on which pocket-label vintage is cited,
  [[TASK-0229.001]]/`documentation/PHASE1_SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md` §2.2).
  TASK-0201's corrected compact-patch null, 1000 draws: p=0.024. Checked
  against two different bars, deliberately, not just the naive one:
  clears a local 2-test Bonferroni bar (0.05/2=0.025) by a hair, but is
  **~3 orders of magnitude short of this program's own register-wide
  multiplicity bar** (≈0.05/228≈0.00022, [[TASK-0161]]/[[TASK-0199]]).
  **Explicit judgment call, stated plainly**: this is *not* counted as a
  new "surviving positive" for the register — the register's own
  standing bar is the one that matters, and this does not clear it. It
  is reported as a real, strong point-estimate margin from a
  never-before-tried method, not inflated into a significance claim the
  project's own convention would reject.
- **PTP1B**: whole-graph AUC 0.610 vs. floor 0.451 — beats floor. Null
  p=0.088 — not significant even locally. **A real internal discrepancy
  disclosed, not smoothed over**: Zheng's own preferred statistic
  (pocket-level ≥50%-overlap hit rate) shows real hit rate (12.5%)
  *below* decoy hit rate (18.3%) on this target — the opposite of
  specificity — even though the softer residue-level AUC clears the
  floor. Both numbers reported side by side in `RESULTS.md`; the
  residue-level AUC is not presented as if it were consistent with the
  pocket-level statistic when it isn't.
