## Why this matters — this is the experiment that settles an open dispute

[[TASK-0158]] found the project's historical scattered null anti-conservative
(4.8× at α=0.05) and replaced it with a compact k-NN ball. The external panel
review reproduced the 4.8× figure independently (5.2×) but found the
**replacement over-corrects**: on a synthetic surface-lining pocket geometry,
actual α under the ball null was 0.013 against a nominal 0.05 — roughly 4×
too conservative, at a 2–2.5× cost in power. The ball is ~30% more compact
than a surface patch and is correctly calibrated *only* in the limit where a
real pocket is a solid ball.

That finding is currently **synthetic-only**. This task measures it on real
apo topologies with the project's own machinery, and it decides something
consequential: [[TASK-0158]]'s corrected re-run removed `dcc_low`'s
Bonferroni significance on both CARDIAC_MYOSIN (p 0.000→0.060) and PTP1B
(p 0.001→0.019), firing the pre-registered falsification statement and
producing [[TASK-0161]]'s "zero positives program-wide" headline. **If the
ball null is measurably over-conservative on real data, that falsification
fired against a mis-specified alternative and the headline needs correcting.**

Note the diagnostic already in hand: [[TASK-0161]] observed *fewer* positives
than α=0.05 chance predicts (0 observed vs ~11.3 expected across 226 cells)
and read it as strength. **A test that produces fewer positives than chance
is, by definition, not operating at its nominal level.** That observation was
already the evidence; this task turns it into a measurement.

## Intent Contract

- Outcome: a measured end-to-end false-positive rate per (target × observable
  × null specification), against a nominal α, with binomial CIs — plus a
  one-line verdict on which null specification is calibrated on real data.
- Why required, not assumed: the pipeline's actual α has never been measured
  end-to-end at protein scale; every p-value the project reports is
  conditioned on a null whose calibration on real, spatially autocorrelated
  score fields is unverified in either direction.

- In Scope:
  - `strength=0` (bit-identical `W`, asserted per [[TASK-0167.001]]'s
    identity test), ≥500 randomly placed distal compact patches per (target,
    observable), each pushed through the **complete** certification chain
    used in .002.
  - The same 3 null specifications: scattered / compact-ball /
    Rg-matched-to-the-drawn-patch.
  - **The calibration statistic itself, and this is the generalizable part:**
    for each null, check whether the *real* labelled pocket's own geometry
    statistic (radius of gyration; secondarily graph spread and mean
    pairwise hop) lies inside the null draws' distribution of that same
    statistic. Report the percentile. A null whose draws are systematically
    more compact than the real pocket is over-conservative; systematically
    less compact, anti-conservative. **This is a direct, cheap, and
    completely general admissibility test that costs one function.**
  - Measure and report the real pocket Rg for all 7 pocket-scoreable targets
    — a number the project needs anyway and does not currently have recorded.

- Out Of Scope:
  - Changing any null in `RESULTS.md`'s already-reported cells. This task
    *measures*; the re-run of affected cells is a separate follow-up, filed
    once this task's verdict is known (do not pre-file the correction — that
    presumes the outcome).
  - Inventing a new null draw. If all three are miscalibrated, that is the
    finding and the fix is a separate task.
  - The LOD — .002.

- Constraints And Invariants:
  - Patches drawn under the same admission criteria as .002's planted patches
    (distal, floor-AUC ≤ 0.5 pre-draw) so the two experiments are directly
    comparable. A specificity measured on easy patches does not bound the LOD
    measured on hard ones.
  - No gate is relaxed, skipped, or reordered.
  - `strength=0` must be verified bit-identical, not assumed — otherwise this
    is not a specificity measurement.

- Planned Validation:
  - **Concordance:** this task's false-positive rate must match .002's
    `strength=0` row within Monte Carlo error. Two independent routes; a
    disagreement is a bug.
  - **Positive control on the calibration statistic itself:** a deliberately
    scattered draw must register as anti-conservative and a deliberately
    over-compact draw (e.g. size/2 nearest neighbours) as over-conservative.
    If the statistic cannot detect miscalibration it is known to have, it
    cannot certify calibration it claims to find.

## Open Questions

- If all three nulls are miscalibrated on real data, what is the right draw?
  Likely candidate: sample from the *empirical* distribution of real pocket
  geometries across the 7 targets rather than any parametric compactness
  model. Out of scope; file if it arises.
- Is α target-dependent? Almost certainly (topology and pocket geometry
  differ). Report per-target; a pooled number is what the paper needs but
  the per-target spread is what tells you whether pooling is legitimate.
- Should the calibration statistic become a fourth invariant class
  (**CALIBRATION**, alongside GAUGE/KNOB/SIGNAL) in
  `INVARIANCE_PROTOCOL.md`? The external review recommends it: *a null is
  only admissible if the real label is exchangeable with the null draws
  under the statistic the test depends on.* Decide once this task has data;
  it is the most transferable methodological output the project has produced
  and would be a genuine contribution independent of the allostery result.
