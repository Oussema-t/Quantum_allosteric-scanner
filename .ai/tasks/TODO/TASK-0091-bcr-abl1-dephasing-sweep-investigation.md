### The real, still-open question

BCR_ABL1's real run produced `AUC_ctqw_mean = 0.525` (chance) but
`AUC_heat_mean = 0.731` (well above chance) on the **same** `H_new`
operator (`apo`, `enm_cutoff=8.0`). The review's own non-findings section
already ran the obvious first check — is this just proximity in a
different disguise? — and found: **not conclusively**. On the review's
synthetic 170-residue globule, `heat` occupation correlates with
−Euclidean distance from seed at Spearman **+0.589** (weaker than CTQW's
0.853, but *not immune*), and a distal-pocket AUC for `heat` is **0.048**
— just as anti-correlated as CTQW's 0.035. So ground-state localization
is *not* proven to escape the proximity confound on the synthetic case —
but BCR_ABL1's real 0.731 is a real, unexplained, and potentially
genuine result on an *actually distal* pocket (myristoyl site, ~25 Å from
the active site) that the synthetic case does not settle either way.
**This is the one open line of inquiry from the review that is not
proximity in disguise, per the review's own sequencing note** — but it
must be tested, not assumed.
